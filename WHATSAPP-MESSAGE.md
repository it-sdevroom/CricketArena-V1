# WhatsApp messages to share the app

Copy the one you need. WhatsApp turns `*text*` into **bold**, so the asterisks
are meant to be there.

The link used throughout is the **latest release** page rather than a numbered
file. It always points at the newest version, so these messages stay correct
when the app is updated — no need to rewrite them each time.

---

## 1. Main message — send to everyone

```
🏏 *PPP4 Summer Sport 2026 — Cricket App*

Our tournament now has its own app. Live scores, points table, fixtures and player stats — all in one place.

📱 *Install on Android*
https://github.com/it-sdevroom/CricketArena-V1/releases/latest

Open the link, download the file ending in *.apk*, then tap it to install.

Your phone will warn you it is from an "unknown source". This is normal for an app not from the Play Store. Tap *Settings*, allow it, then *Install*.

🌐 *No Android? Use the website*
https://it-sdevroom.github.io/CricketArena-V1/

Works on iPhone and computer. Nothing to install.

*What you can do*

👀 *Fans* — follow live scores ball by ball, see the points table, check fixtures. No sign-up needed.

🏏 *Players* — sign up, then More → Register as a player. Pick your team, add your photo. The organiser approves you, and your runs and wickets are recorded for the season.

📋 *Committee* — scorers and organisers get extra tools. Tell me your email after you sign up and I will give you access.

Any problem, message me.
```

## Things worth knowing before you send it

**The "unknown source" warning is expected.** Every app installed outside the
Play Store shows it. Saying so in the message stops people assuming something
is wrong and giving up.

**Nobody has to sign up to watch.** Fans can open the app or the website and
see live scores immediately. Only players and officials need an account, and
saying that up front removes the main reason people ignore a link.

**iPhone users are not left out.** The website is the whole app and works on
any phone. An iPhone build would need a paid Apple developer account, so the
website is the answer for now — and it needs no installation at all.

**Ask for emails, do not guess them.** Roles are assigned to an account that
already exists, so people need to sign up before you can make them a scorer.
